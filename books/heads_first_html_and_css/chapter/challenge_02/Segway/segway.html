Segway'n USA

Documenting Tony's trip around the US on his own Segway!

June 2, 2005

His first day of the trip! You can't believe he finally got everything packed and ready to go. Because he is on a Segway, he wasn't able to bring a whole lot with him:

  • Cell phone
  • ipod
  • digital camera
  • protein bar

Just the essentials.

As Lao Tzu would have said: A journey of a thousand miles beigins with one step Segway.

July 14, 2005
